Veluma publisher

Veluma publisher is the private publishing tool of Veluma Videos (@velumavideos), a YouTube channel of short, data-driven videos.

What it does

The owner of the Veluma Videos channel runs it on their own computer to upload the channel's own videos to YouTube through the YouTube Data API, set their titles, descriptions and playlists, schedule their publication, and check their status.

It is not offered to anyone else. Nobody other than the channel owner signs in to it, and it acts only on the Veluma Videos channel.
